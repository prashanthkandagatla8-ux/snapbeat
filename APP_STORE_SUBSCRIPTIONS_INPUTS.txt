================================================================================
SNAPBEAT STUDIO — APP STORE CONNECT INPUTS SPECIFICATION
================================================================================
Use this document as your exact copy-paste guide when setting up Auto-Renewable
Subscriptions and Consumable In-App Purchases (Credit Top-Ups) in App Store Connect.
All naming consistently reflects our official app name: "SnapBeat Studio".

NOTE ON PRODUCT IDENTIFIERS:
Because Apple App Store permanently reserves previously deleted product IDs,
SnapBeat Studio uses the updated namespace "snapbeat_studio_pro_*".
The app's SubscriptionManager also maintains backwards-compatible fallbacks for
legacy IDs ("snapbeat_pro_*").

PART 4: APP STORE REVIEW INFORMATION & NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Paste this into the App Review Information "Notes" field:

```
APP REVIEW NOTES FOR SNAPBEAT STUDIO v1.0.7:

1. MONETIZATION MODEL:
- Free users have FULL CREATIVE FREEDOM in SnapBeat Studio: All 14 cinematic templates, transitions, aspect ratios (9:16, 1:1, 16:9), beat-sync algorithms, audio trimming, and title card creators are completely unlocked and free.
- Free users render at 360p Standard resolution with an embossed 3D watermark processed on a shared standard queue.
- Pro subscriptions (Weekly, Monthly, Annual) unlock 720p HD and 1080p Master exports, eliminate watermarks, grant cloud priority queue processing, and provide render credits for instant Google Cloud Run serverless renders (8 credits/wk, 40 credits/mo, 432 credits/yr).

2. HOW TO TEST IN-APP PURCHASES:
- Launch SnapBeat Studio.
- Tap 'HD 720p PRO' or '1080p MASTER PRO' in the Quality Selector, or tap 'START CREATING REEL' with a Pro resolution selected.
- The Retro Metal Subscription Dialog appears showing Weekly Pass ($1.99), Monthly VIP ($4.99), and Annual VIP ($24.99).
- Tapping any plan triggers the StoreKit sandbox purchase sheet.
- Restoring purchases is accessible via the 'Restore Purchases' link at the bottom of the paywall dialog.

3. PRIVACY POLICY & TERMS:
- Privacy Policy: https://snapbeat.app/privacy
- Terms of Use: https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/

4. BACKEND API:
- Secure production endpoint: https://api.snapbeat.app (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
- No sensitive user personal data is collected or retained.
```
