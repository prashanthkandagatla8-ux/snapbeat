SnapBeat — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 8, 2026 | Last Updated: September 8, 2026

Summary: SnapBeat processes only the photos and music you explicitly select to generate your music video. Your uploaded files are processed ephemerally on our secure server and are permanently deleted immediately after download. We never sell, share, or use your photos for advertising or public AI training.

1. Information We Process

A. User-Selected Media

SnapBeat only accesses the specific photos and audio track that you select using the Android system picker. The app does not scan or access unselected files on your device.

Ephemeral Processing: Files are sent securely via TLS/HTTPS to our dedicated rendering backend. Once your video is generated and downloaded to your gallery, all temporary uploaded photos and audio files are automatically deleted.

B. AI Smart Arrangement

When using AI Smart Arrangement, visual attributes (such as tempo synchronization and image vibrance) are analyzed solely to sequence photos for your video. Your content is never used to train public models.

2. Permissions Requested

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required to send rendering jobs to our server.
  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Optional. Sends a notification when your background video is ready in your gallery.
  • android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE: Ensures background renders finish even if you switch apps.

3. Data Sharing & Security

We do not share, sell, or monetize user data. All transmissions utilize industry-standard TLS encryption.
