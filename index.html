🔒 SnapBeat Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 8, 2026

1. Ephemeral Media Processing

SnapBeat processes only the specific photos and music track you upload. Files are sent via encrypted HTTPS to our dedicated rendering backend solely to synchronize beats, arrange visuals, and produce your MP4 video.

2. Automatic Deletion

Your uploaded photos and music are processed temporarily and automatically deleted immediately after download. We never store, archive, or retain your personal media.

3. AI Smart Arrangement

Visual attributes (vibrance, contrast, tempo drop markers) are analyzed solely to sequence photos for the video climax. We never train public AI models on your photos.

4. No Tracking or Selling

We do not sell, share, or monetize user data with third parties or advertisers.
