1. Introduction & Commitment

SnapBeat ("we", "our", or "us") provides a high-performance, beat-synced video generator for mobile devices. We believe creative tools must respect your digital sovereignty and personal privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains how media files and technical data are handled when using the SnapBeat mobile application and its cloud rendering infrastructure. This document adheres strictly to the Google Play 2026 Developer Privacy & Data Safety Requirements, General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

2. Data Collection: What We Request

SnapBeat only accesses files that you deliberately choose through system pickers:

  • User-Selected Photos: Images you specifically select from your device storage or gallery for inclusion in your beat-synced reel.
  • User-Selected Audio Files: Audio files you pick from local storage or choose from our pre-packaged, offline royalty-free sound catalog.
What we NEVER collect: We do not collect names, phone numbers, email addresses, contact lists, GPS location, microphone recordings, biometric facial geometry, or unique advertising identifiers (GAID/IDFA).

3. Data Usage & Cloud Video Assembly

When you tap "RENDER REEL", your chosen photos, audio snippet, and style parameters are transmitted via encrypted HTTPS/TLS 1.3 to our cloud rendering engine.

This data is used solely and exclusively for:

  • Transient Beat Detection: Detecting tempo (BPM) and peak transients in the audio waveform to calculate transition cut-points.
  • Frame Stitching & Encoding: Assembling the video sequence, applying beat-synced zooms, transitions, or title cards.
  • Stream Return: Delivering the finished MP4 video file back to your device storage.

4. Ephemeral Storage & Automatic Deletion

SnapBeat practices strict ephemeral data processing:

  • Immediate Server Deletion: The rendering worker executes with delete_after=true. The moment video rendering finishes and the resulting file is returned, all input photos, music clips, and intermediate artifacts are permanently erased from server memory and scratch storage.
  • Zero Persistent Storage: We maintain no database of user photos, no cloud vaults, and no persistent media backups.
  • User-Controlled Local Video: Generated MP4 videos downloaded to your phone are stored locally in your app storage or camera roll, completely within your control. You may delete them at any time.

5. Third-Party Sharing & AI Training Prohibition

We hold a strict zero-compromise position regarding data sharing:

  • Zero Data Sharing or Selling: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your photos, songs, or videos to any third parties, brokers, or marketing networks.
  • Prohibition on AI Training: Media uploaded for video compilation is never ingested, saved, or repurposed for training artificial intelligence, computer vision, or machine learning algorithms.

6. Tracking, Analytics & Advertising

SnapBeat contains:

  • ❌ Zero Ad Networks: No advertising SDKs or programmatic advertising hooks.
  • ❌ Zero Analytics SDKs: No Google Analytics for Firebase, Facebook Pixel, AppsFlyer, or third-party telemetry tools.
  • ❌ Zero Cross-App Tracking: No tracking of user identity or device behaviors across external applications.

7. Android Device Permissions

SnapBeat requests only the bare minimum permissions required for core functions:

  • Photo Picker / Media Images (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES): Used solely to allow you to select photos for your reel. We utilize the Android system Photo Picker which limits access strictly to the items you explicitly select.
  • Audio Selection (READ_MEDIA_AUDIO): Used solely to allow you to select your preferred background soundtrack from local device storage.
  • Network Access (INTERNET): Used solely to transmit media to the ephemeral cloud rendering worker and stream back your generated video file over secure TLS.

8. Children's Privacy (COPPA / GDPR)

SnapBeat is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA/UK). Because our service requires no accounts, collects no personal identifiers, and deletes all processed media immediately upon render completion, it is safe and compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and GDPR Article 8.

9. Security Architecture

All communications between the SnapBeat mobile client and cloud rendering cluster are encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS). Rendering jobs run in isolated, sandboxed compute containers with automated sanitization hooks that purge local scratch disks immediately upon job conclusion.
